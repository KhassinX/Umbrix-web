Private messaging.
Actually private.

Hermod is end-to-end encrypted 1:1 messaging built natively for Apple devices. No servers of ours, no account, no phone number, no email — and nothing collected about you. We could not read your messages if we wanted to, and we designed it so we never have to be trusted.

In development · Coming to iPhone, iPad, Mac & Watch

Why Hermod is different

End-to-end encrypted, always

Every message is encrypted on your device and decrypted only on the recipient's. Signal-style protocol built on Apple's CryptoKit; private keys never leave your device.

No servers holding your messages

We operate zero servers. Encrypted messages travel through an ephemeral relay that deletes them on delivery. There is no archive — anywhere — to request, leak, or breach.

No identity harvest

Your identity is a cryptographic key generated on your device. No phone number, no email, no username directory. You share it in person, by QR code or by link.

Honest by design

We publish what we can see, what we can't, and what protections are best-effort. Read the transparency report and our warrant canary.

Zero data collection — verified, not promised

Hermod ships with an empty App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs of any kind. The privacy policy is short because there is genuinely nothing to disclose — read it.
