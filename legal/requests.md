Umbrix is neutral software. It does not promote or oppose any cause, government, or political position. KHASSINX LLC complies with applicable local law. This page explains how we handle the two kinds of request an authority may send.

## Requests for data or surveillance

There is nothing to hand over. Umbrix has no accounts, no phone numbers, no user directory, and no servers of ours in the message path. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and the ephemeral relay deletes them on delivery, so no archive of content, metadata, or contact relationships exists on our side to produce.

We cannot provide what we do not have. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/), the [Transparency Report](/legal/transparency/), and the [Warrant Canary](/legal/canary/).

## Requests to make Umbrix unavailable in a country

Where a competent authority determines that Umbrix should not be available in its territory, it can ask us directly — without escalating to Apple or to court. We treat this as a routine compliance matter, not a dispute.

**A valid request includes all three of:**

1. The competent authority, identified (the body and its jurisdiction).
2. The legal basis cited — the specific law, regulation, or ruling of that jurisdiction that Umbrix is said to infringe.
3. The country or territory affected.

**How to send it:** email [`legal@khassinx.com`](mailto:legal@khassinx.com) with the subject `[GEO-REMOVAL] <country> — <authority>`.

**Verification.** Before acting, we confirm the request through the authority's own official published channel — we do not act on the incoming email alone. This protects people in a country from having the app removed by someone impersonating their government.

**What we do.** Once verified, we restrict the app's availability in that territory through the App Store. This is reversible: if the authority later withdraws its position, availability is restored. We record the action on the [availability page](/legal/availability/). We do not weaken the encryption for any market.

We aim to acknowledge requests within five business days.
