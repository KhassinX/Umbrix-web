Support

Every message gets a reply from a person. No bots, no ticket black holes.

How to reach us

Email support@khassinx.com. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days.

We provide support in English and Español only. Messages in other languages may go unanswered or receive a reply in English.

What to include

  • Your device and OS version (for example, "iPhone 17, iOS 26.5").
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Never include the content of your private conversations. We don't need it to help you, and screenshots of chats can expose things you meant to keep private — crop them first.

Purchases and refunds

Umbrix's core messaging is free. Any optional purchases are processed by Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Frequently asked questions

Is Umbrix available yet?

Umbrix is in development. It has not shipped on the App Store yet; this site will announce availability when it does.

Can you recover my messages or reset my Umbrix ID?

No — and that's by design. Umbrix has no accounts and no servers of ours: your identity keys and your message history exist only on your devices. If you delete the app or lose your device, we have no copy to restore. Nobody does.

Can you read a conversation to help me with a problem?

No. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and we operate no servers in the message path, so there is nothing on our side to look at. Describe the problem in your own words instead.

How do I report someone?

Use the block and report tools inside the app. Blocking takes effect immediately on your device. If you report unlawful content or conduct, our review works with what you voluntarily provide — see the Terms of Service for how enforcement works in an end-to-end encrypted app.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Not here — use the dedicated channel: security@khassinx.com. Details at Security & Responsible Disclosure.

In what languages is support offered?

English and Español. The app itself may support more languages over time, but support conversations happen in these two only.
